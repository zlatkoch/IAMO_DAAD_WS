01

Airport to hotel

Most people land at Skopje International (SKP) on Sunday 27 September. Two ways in — pick based on your arrival time.

Shuttle bus — Erak Transporter

The official, recommended transfer. Check live times at eraktransporter.com.

  • ~200 MKD (€3.30–€5.00) one way
  • Buy at the airport counter (08:00–20:00) or from the driver overnight
  • Keep your receipt — it's reimbursable
  • Get off at Holiday Inn / Macedonia Square (35–40 min); it's a 400 m walk to the hotel from there

Taxi check policy first

Official white taxis wait right outside arrivals.

  • Flat rate: 1,500 MKD (≈ €25) to the city center
  • Not reimbursable unless pre-authorized
  • The one exception DAAD allows: a late-night arrival when no shuttle is running

Hotel & the streets around it

Best Western Hotel Turist

Gjuro Strugar 11, Skopje 1000 (11 Oktomvri area)

Check-in from 14:00, check-out by 12:00 noon. +389 2 328 9111.

The hotel sits directly on pedestrian Macedonia Street, facing the Mother Teresa Memorial House — free entry, closed Mondays. Walk five minutes north up the same street and you're at Macedonia Square and the 15th-century Stone Bridge. For Debar Maalo, Skopje's bohemian quarter, keep going to the square, turn west onto Orce Nikolov, and continue another 10–12 minutes.

SCHEMATIC — NOT TO SCALE. Tap any restaurant below for the real pin.
